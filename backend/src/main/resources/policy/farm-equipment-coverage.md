# Farm Equipment Coverage Form NSF-EQ-01 (synthetic sample wording)

### FEC-1.1 Insuring agreement
We pay for direct physical loss of or damage to covered equipment caused by a covered cause of loss during the policy period, subject to the deductible and the limit shown in the declarations.

### FEC-1.2 Covered equipment
Covered equipment means the self-propelled and towed farm machinery, irrigation pivots, grain dryers and utility vehicles listed by serial number on the equipment schedule, including permanently attached parts and electronics.

### FEC-1.3 Newly acquired equipment
Equipment acquired during the policy period is covered for 30 days, up to 25 percent of the scheduled value, provided you report it to us within those 30 days.

### FEC-2.1 Covered causes of loss
Covered causes of loss are collision, overturn, fire, lightning, hail, windstorm, flood, theft and vandalism, unless an exclusion applies.

### FEC-2.2 Hail and windstorm
Hail and windstorm damage is covered when it is consistent with a weather event recorded in the county on or near the date of loss. Pre-existing hail damage noted at inspection is not covered.

### FEC-2.3 Flood and water damage
Flood damage is covered for equipment that was not left within a mapped floodway after a flood warning had been issued for that location.

### FEC-2.4 Lightning and power surge
Damage to controllers, monitors and wiring caused by a lightning strike or a power surge from a lightning strike is covered.

### FEC-3.1 Wear and tear exclusion
We do not pay for wear and tear, gradual deterioration, rust, corrosion or the cost of replacing parts that have reached the end of their normal service life.

### FEC-3.2 Mechanical breakdown exclusion
We do not pay for mechanical or electrical breakdown, including engine, transmission, gearbox and hydraulic failure, unless the Mechanical Breakdown Endorsement FEC-8.1 is attached. Resulting fire or collision damage is covered.

### FEC-3.3 Intentional acts exclusion
We do not pay for loss caused intentionally by you, a family member, an employee or anyone acting with your consent.

### FEC-3.4 Unattended theft exclusion
We do not pay for theft of equipment left unattended away from the insured premises overnight unless it was locked, immobilized or fitted with a working GPS tracking device.

### FEC-3.5 Overloading and misuse
We do not pay for damage caused by operating equipment beyond the manufacturer's rated capacity or for a purpose it was not designed for.

### FEC-4.1 Notice of loss
You must notify us or your agent as soon as practicable and no later than 30 days after you discover the loss. Late notice does not void coverage unless the delay prejudices our investigation.

### FEC-4.2 Police report for theft and vandalism
For theft or vandalism you must report the loss to law enforcement within 48 hours and give us the report number.

### FEC-4.3 Proof of loss
Within 60 days of our request you must send a signed proof of loss with photos of the damage, the serial number, and a repair or replacement estimate from a dealer or repair shop.

### FEC-4.4 Protect the equipment from further damage
You must take reasonable steps to protect the equipment from further damage. We pay the reasonable cost of those steps as part of the claim.

### FEC-4.5 Inspection and records
You must let us inspect the damaged equipment before repair and give us maintenance records, engine hour readings and purchase documents when we ask.

### FEC-5.1 Valuation
We pay the actual cash value of the damaged part or machine at the time of loss. Equipment under five model years old is valued at replacement cost with like kind and quality.

### FEC-5.2 Deductible
We pay only the amount of loss above the deductible shown in the declarations. One deductible applies per occurrence.

### FEC-5.3 Limit of insurance
The most we pay for loss to any one machine is its scheduled value, and the most we pay in one occurrence is the limit shown in the declarations.

### FEC-6.1 Concealment, misrepresentation or fraud
This coverage is void for a claim if you or anyone insured intentionally concealed or misrepresented a material fact, including the cause, date or amount of loss.

### FEC-6.2 Appraisal
If we and you disagree on the amount of loss, either may demand an appraisal. Each party selects an appraiser and the two select an umpire.

### FEC-6.3 Recovered property
If stolen equipment is recovered after we pay, you may keep it and return our payment less the cost of repairing any damage.

### FEC-7.1 Payment of claims
We pay covered loss within 30 days after we receive an acceptable proof of loss and agree on the amount, or after an appraisal award.

### FEC-7.2 Fast track settlement
Claims under 25,000 dollars with photos and a dealer estimate may be settled without a field inspection at our option.

### FEC-8.1 Mechanical Breakdown Endorsement
When attached, mechanical and electrical breakdown of covered equipment is covered for machines under 10 model years old and under 8,000 engine hours, subject to a separate 2,500 dollar deductible.

### FEC-8.2 Harvest Downtime Endorsement
When attached, we pay rental of substitute equipment for up to 21 days when covered damage occurs during planting or harvest, up to 500 dollars per day.

### FEC-8.3 GPS Tracking Credit
Equipment fitted with an active GPS tracking device receives a premium credit, and the unattended theft exclusion FEC-3.4 does not apply to it.
